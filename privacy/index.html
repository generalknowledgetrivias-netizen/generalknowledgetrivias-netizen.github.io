Privacy Policy

Trivia Shorts Poster · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Trivia Shorts Poster ("the App") is a private tool operated by General Knowledge Trivias to publish its own trivia videos to its own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube and Instagram) on a schedule. The App is not offered to the public.

Information we access

  • When the account owner connects a TikTok account, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (such as the account's open ID and display name) through TikTok's official Login Kit, with the scopes user.info.basic and video.publish.
  • The App reads video files and captions that the account owner has prepared in their own Google Drive and Google Sheets.

How we use it

This information is used only to upload and publish videos to the account owner's own accounts and to record whether each post succeeded. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is only sent to the platform it belongs to (for example, a video sent to TikTok is sent through TikTok's Content Posting API).

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on a private computer controlled by the account owner. They are never published or shared. The login callback page on this website only displays the authorization code in the visitor's own browser; it does not send or store it anywhere.

Data deletion

You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services. To request deletion of any stored data, email generalknowledgetrivias@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
